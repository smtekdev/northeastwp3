Ask a Question
Do you train at our facility?

Yes. We bring instructors and materials to your site, schedule around your shifts, and train groups of any size.

Are your certificates recognized?

Our OSHA outreach courses issue official DOL cards. Other courses issue certificates with CEU credit where applicable.

How fast can you start?

Most assessments are scheduled within a week. Urgent requests after an incident or citation are prioritized.

Which industries do you serve?

Construction, manufacturing, warehousing, utilities, healthcare and municipal operations across New England.

Can we take courses online?

Yes. Our learning center offers self-paced online courses with progress tracking and instant certificates.
